## A useful starting point
Generative AI is software that learns patterns from examples and uses those patterns to produce new content. That content can be text, images, audio, or code. It is useful to think of it as a pattern-based collaborator whose output still needs review.

## What happens when you send a prompt?
For a language model, your text is split into tokens: small pieces of text. The model uses the context to estimate which tokens should follow. It repeats that process to produce an answer. Fluent writing is a result of that process; it is not evidence that every statement is true.

## Try a small, low-stakes task
1. Pick a paragraph you wrote.
2. Ask an assistant to make it easier for a beginner to understand.
3. Compare the result with your original.
4. Check that the meaning stayed the same.

<Callout>Start with material you can judge yourself. Avoid uploading private or sensitive information before you understand the service’s data settings.</Callout>

## What it can help with
- Drafting an outline you can revise.
- Explaining an unfamiliar concept at several levels.
- Suggesting test cases for a small piece of code.
- Generating alternatives when you feel stuck.

## What you still bring
You choose the goal, supply context, and decide whether the result is useful. For factual work, verify claims against original sources. For code, read it and test its behavior. For creative work, check originality and fit.
