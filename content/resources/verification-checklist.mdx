## What it does
This checklist turns “check the answer” into a few concrete actions.

## Before you use an answer
- Identify its important factual claims.
- Open each original source; do not rely on citation formatting.
- Confirm that the source supports the claim.
- Check dates and qualifications.
- Test instructions or code when you can do so safely.

## How to use it
Keep the downloaded checklist beside your research notes. Record any unresolved claim and leave it out of public work until it is verified.
