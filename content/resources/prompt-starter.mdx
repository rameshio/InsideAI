## What it does
This prompt template helps you describe your task before asking an assistant to do the work. It is a starting point, not a guarantee of a correct answer.

## How to use it
1. Copy the template below or download the text file.
2. Replace each bracketed field with your own details.
3. Send it to your chosen assistant.
4. Review the result, then refine one instruction at a time.
