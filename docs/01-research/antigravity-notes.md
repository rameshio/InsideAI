## 5. Risks and Open Questions for the Owner

### Critical Risks to Avoid
1. **The Daily Update Friction Trap (5–10 min/day limit):**
   - *Risk:* If publishing a daily update requires opening VS Code, running Git commands, or waiting 3 minutes for a Vercel build, you will abandon it within two weeks.
   - *Recommendation:* Daily updates must be editable via a zero-friction interface—either a simple Markdown folder with a GitHub web editor, or a lightweight free headless CMS (like Keystatic or a Notion database connected via API) that you can update from your phone.
2. **Mobile Speed Decay from YouTube Embeds:**
   - *Risk:* Embedding 6–8 live YouTube iframes on a page downloads over 3MB of JavaScript and severely lags mobile browsers (especially in Instagram's in-app browser).
   - *Recommendation:* Use **Lite YouTube Embeds** (static WebP thumbnails with a play button; the iframe only loads when tapped).
3. **The Fragile Instagram API:**
   - *Risk:* Relying on Meta’s Instagram Graph API to automatically pull Reels is notoriously fragile—tokens expire every 60 days, and Meta frequently blocks personal accounts.
   - *Recommendation:* Automate YouTube Shorts via the public YouTube RSS/API (which is 100% free and never expires). Manually cross-link Instagram Reels with a simple link attribute.

### Open Questions for You:
1. **Domain Preference:** Which domain do you prefer (~$10–12/year)?
   - `insideai.tools` (matches @insideaitoolbox, strong for directory/tutorials)
   - `insideai.vision` (matches your Instagram brand handle)
   - `insideai.xyz` (popular modern tech TLD, clean and cheap)
2. **Newsletter Platform:** Do you already have a newsletter tool in mind? (I strongly recommend **Beehiiv** or **Substack**—both have 100% free starter tiers and handle subscriber delivery without server setup).
3. **Future Video Format:** Will you remain 100% focused on Shorts/Reels, or do you plan to introduce longer 10-minute YouTube tutorials later? (Our proposed layout works for both, but knowing this helps optimize the video player ratio).

### 4. Impact of Owner's Clarification: "3D" = Scroll-Driven Video Scrubbing

> Added 2026-10-13 following the owner's note in `docs/00-brief.md`: *"by '3D website' I mean scroll-driven animation: as you scroll the page, a video plays frame by frame in sync with the scroll (scroll forward = video moves forward, scroll back = it rewinds)."*

This clarification completely reframes the engineering challenge. Previously, all three AIs assumed static tactile renders. Here is how this changes my technical and design recommendations:

#### A. The Required Technique: Canvas Image Sequence (Apple-Style), NOT `<video>` Tag Scrubbing
- **Why Native `<video>` Tag Scrubbing Fails on Web:**
  A common developer trap is binding scroll position directly to video playback time (`video.currentTime = scrollProgress * duration`). On modern browsers—especially mobile Safari and Instagram's in-app browser—this results in **disastrous stuttering and dropped frames**. Standard video compression uses temporal compression (I-frames every 1–2 seconds, with P/B predictive frames between). Seeking backward forces the browser's hardware video decoder to reconstruct frames backwards from the last keyframe on every single scroll tick, causing lag, frame skipping, and massive CPU spikes.
- **The Battle-Tested Apple Technique:**
  Export the 3D animation as a sequence of **optimized still frames (WebP or AVIF)**. Render the active frame onto an HTML5 `<canvas>` element via `ctx.drawImage()`, synchronized via **GSAP ScrollTrigger**:
  - The browser preloads the lightweight frames.
  - As the user scrolls, JavaScript immediately paints the exact matching frame index to the canvas in 16ms (60fps).
  - This guarantees **true 60fps bidirectional scrubbing**, zero decoder lag, and instant, buttery-smooth reverse playback.

#### B. Where on the Site It Belongs
- **Homepage Hero Section ONLY (A Pinned 1.5x–2x Viewport Scroller):**
  - The hero section pins in place for approximately 1.5x to 2x the viewport scroll height.
  - As the user scrolls, the camera pushes in, the 3D toolbox opens, and the text fades in. Once the animation completes, the section unpins and normal scrolling resumes into Today's Pulse and the video library.
  - **Do NOT make the whole site a scroll-scrubbed video tunnel:** If visitors coming from an Instagram Reel or YouTube Short have to scroll through a mandatory 10-second video just to find a prompt or read Today's Pulse, bounce rates will exceed 80%.
  - **Lesson and Update pages must remain 100% instantaneous, static, and scroll-clean.**

#### C. Mobile Performance & Instagram In-App Browser Reality
- **The Instagram In-App Browser Constraint:**
  Over 80% of your initial traffic arrives via Instagram's in-app browser (`WKWebView` on iOS). This webview has strict memory caps, will crash or force-reload if GPU memory spikes, and often operates over cellular data.
- **Mitigation Architecture:**
  1. **Adaptive Frame Loading:**
     - **Desktop:** ~90–120 frames @ 1080p (approx. 4–5 MB total, preloading the first 15 frames immediately and streaming the rest).
     - **Mobile / Instagram Webview:** Serve a lightweight downsampled sequence: ~45–60 frames @ 720p/540p (WebP quality 70, ~15–20 KB/frame ≈ 1 MB total).
  2. **Instagram Dynamic Viewport Bug (`100vh` fix):** Instagram's in-app browser dynamically resizes its top and bottom URL chrome on scroll. Using CSS `100vh` causes severe layout jumps. We must use `100dvh` (dynamic viewport height) or a fixed JavaScript resize lock for the pinned canvas.
  3. **Reduced-Motion Fallback:** For users with `prefers-reduced-motion` enabled or slow mobile connections (`navigator.connection.saveData`), skip the canvas scrubbing entirely and display a single, gorgeous static hero frame with standard CSS entrance transitions.

#### D. How to Produce the Animation WITHOUT Looking AI-Generated
The owner's rule is strict: *"must NOT look AI-generated: no purple/blue glowing gradients, no robot heads, no glowing brains, no stock neural network art. Prefer crafted, tactile 3D with depth, real lighting, soft shadows, objects you could touch."*

- **Why AI Video Generators (Runway, Luma, Sora) Will FAIL:**
  Generative AI video tools produce morphing geometry, temporal jitter, wobbly edges, and plastic uncanny-valley textures. When a video is scrubbed frame-by-frame by a human user, AI hallucinations and temporal inconsistencies become painfully glaring. It will instantly scream "cheap AI-generated video."
- **The Correct Production Route: Real 3D Software (Blender or Cinema 4D):**
  - **Tool:** Blender (100% free, open-source, industry-standard).
  - **The Concept: "The InsideAI Physical Toolbox Unboxing"**
