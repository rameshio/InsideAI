- **What a "daily update" is:** short post — headline + 2–3 lines on one AI news item or tip (for now; may grow later)
- **Look:** 3D style, but it must NOT look AI-generated. Avoid the generic "AI website" look: no purple/blue glowing gradients, no robot heads or glowing brains, no stock "neural network" art, no generic AI-template layouts. Prefer crafted, tactile 3D (depth, real lighting, soft shadows, objects you could almost touch) that feels designed by a human. Keep it consistent with the InsideAI logo colors.
